## Reading the knowledge

Identify proposed decisions, current implementation, superseded information,
open questions, and deferred direction. Source and verified runtime evidence
establish current behavior; the human establishes product intent and acceptance.
Reports explain changes and validation. Preserve that distinction when
reconciling a page.

No product architecture or completed work is supplied in this starter. The
coding agent maintains these explanations as the project evolves.
